# A simple manual invoice follow-up

These examples are for ordinary business communication. Adapt them to your actual terms and jurisdiction; they are not legal advice and cannot guarantee payment. Do not threaten fees, penalties, service suspension, public disclosure, or legal action unless you have verified the facts and your rights.

## Before sending a reminder

Check the invoice number, amount, currency, due date, previous payments and credits. Confirm that you have the right contact and a secure way for the client to pay. If the amount or terms are disputed, resolve the discrepancy rather than repeatedly sending the same demand. Keep private invoice records in an access-controlled location.

## Optional manual log

Keep a separate record for each invoice. This is a manual list, not a feature in the invoice spreadsheet.

| Invoice | Due date | Amount due | Last contact | Next action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ID] | [date] | [amount/currency] | [date or none] | [action/date] | [open / partial / paid / disputed] |

## Three editable notes

**Before the due date** — Subject: Invoice [number] due [date]

Hi [name], a quick note that invoice [number] for [amount/currency] is due on [date]. The invoice is attached or available at [secure link]. If you need anything to process it, please let me know. Thank you, [name].

**After the due date** — Subject: Checking on invoice [number]

Hi [name], could you confirm receipt and the expected payment date for invoice [number], due [date], with [amount/currency] outstanding? If payment has already been sent or the invoice needs correction, please let me know. Thank you, [name].

**Partial payment or question** — Subject: Invoice [number] balance

Hi [name], thank you for the payment of [amount] on [date]. My records show a remaining balance of [amount/currency]. Could you confirm whether that matches your records and when the balance is expected? I can resend the invoice if useful. Thank you, [name].
